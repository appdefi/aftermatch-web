What is Aftermatch?

Between the top flights, divisions below, cups and tournaments, there is more football than anyone can keep up with.

A league you've always wanted to check out. An FA Cup third round that crams 32 fixtures into four days.

Aftermatch scores every finished match on drama, stakes and spectacle, and points you to the few that earn 90 minutes of your time.

All without giving away what happened.

One number.

Every completed match gets a score from 0 to 100.

No spoilers.

The listings show the fixture and the score. Nothing else.

Growing coverage.

More competitions and past seasons are added over time.

How scoring works

When a match finishes, we run it through the scoring model. It looks at how the likely result shifted across the 90 minutes, when those shifts happened, how long the outcome stayed in doubt, and how the finish compared to what the bookmakers expected beforehand.

Goals, cards, penalties and saves feed in as well, alongside rivalry and whatever was riding on the result.

Late drama counts for more than early drama. A big occasion raises the score of a good match, though it can't rescue a flat one, and a single wild moment in an otherwise quiet game only goes so far. All of it comes out as one number between 0 and 100.
